## API research (warcraft.wiki.gg, read 2026-10-31, retail 12.x)

- `GetAchievementNumCriteria(achievementID)` -> numCriteria. https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_GetAchievementNumCriteria
- `GetAchievementCriteriaInfo(achievementID, criteriaIndex [, countHidden])` -> `criteriaString, criteriaType, completed, quantity, reqQuantity, charName, flags, assetID, quantityString, criteriaID, eligible, duration, elapsed`. https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_GetAchievementCriteriaInfo . A progress-bar achievement has one criterion with `reqQuantity > 0`.
- `C_MajorFactions.GetRenownRewardsForLevel(majorFactionID, renownLevel)` -> list of `{ renownRewardID, uiOrder, isAccountUnlock, itemID?, spellID?, mountID?, transmogID?, transmogSetID?, titleMaskID?, transmogIllusionSourceID?, icon?, name?, description?, rewardType? (12.0.0), isCollected? (12.0.5) }`, AllowedWhenUntainted. https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_MajorFactions.GetRenownRewardsForLevel . **This is the only client source of reputation reward ids**; classic factions have none.
- `C_Reputation.GetFactionParagonInfo(factionID)` -> `currentValue, threshold, rewardQuestID, hasRewardPending, tooLowLevelForParagon, paragonStorageLevel` (12.0.0), MayReturnNothing; `currentValue` carries earned cycles as a prefix (`value % threshold` is the real progress). https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_Reputation.GetFactionParagonInfo
- `debugstack([thread,] [start [, count1 [, count2]]])` -> stack text; takes a coroutine. https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_debugstack
- `GetMerchantItemCostItem(index, itemIndex)` -> `itemTexture, itemValue, itemLink, currencyName`; `itemLink` is nil for a currency, so a merchant price gives a currency **name, not an id**. https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_GetMerchantItemCostItem (why vendor prices are deferred, D184).
- Limits: no client API lists what a container (cage, chest) can contain; no API lists set pieces missing per transmog set without a full `C_TransmogSets` walk (deferred, D212).

## D. Player taste

- D189 Q: What is taste? A: per Account, over the last 60 days, per **category** (`event`, `chain`, `achievement`, `rare`, `collection` for ideas; `preset:<topic>` for presets; `surprise`): `picked` (Requests), `finished` (plans whose progress has no remaining steps and at least one done), `doneSteps`, `skippedSteps`, `wins` (E). Category of a plan: its Request's `suggestion.kind` or `topic`; a local plan (no Request, e.g. a chain Quick plan) by its progress `source`. Reason: built only from what the player did.
- D190 Q: Which plan does a progress post belong to? A: progress gains optional `generatedAt` (the plan's stamp, already in the session: Session.lua:476): addon `ProgressTable` adds it, Companion `session_progress`/`progress_payload` pass it, `cleanProgress` accepts it (absent from 0.9 clients). Plans get a `generated_at` column (backfilled from `body_json`); the server joins progress to the plan by `generated_at` and Character. Reason: today a progress post cannot name its plan, so finished vs dropped cannot be counted.
- D191 Q: How is it used? A: per category with `picked >= 3`: `rate = (finished + wins + 1) / (picked + 2)`; `bonus = round((rate - 0.5) × 30)` clamped to ±15; a category with `skippedSteps > 2 × doneSteps` gets at least -5. Applied to Suggestions scores (before the per-rule cut) and Ready now scores. `get_ready_now.taste` shows the table and the bonus per category. Reason: small nudges; an event on now (100) still beats a liked collection idea (40 + 15).
- D192 Q: Ideas shown but never picked? A: not counted in 0.10: the addon does not report which ideas were shown. Reason: needs a new upload field; picks and finishes are enough to start.

## E. Wins (success story)

- D193 Q: What is a win? A: something a plan promised that **game data** later shows newly obtained: mount (`mounts`), toy (`toys`), pet species (`pets`), achievement (`achievements`/`achievementsMine`), item (count from 0 to > 0 in `bags`/`bank`/`reagentBank`/`warband`), quest (`questsDone`). Not the Done button, not progress. Reason: owner, game data is truth.
- D194 Q: Which targets does a plan promise? A: tokens in plan `rewards`, step `rewards`, `contains` (F) of kinds mount, toy, pet, ach, item; plus the `complete` of the **last** step when its kind is quest, mount, toy, pet or achievement. A container item with `contains` is not a target itself (its contents are); without `contains` it is. Currency is never a win. Reason: what the card showed as "you get".
- D195 Q: Which plans are open? A: plans linked to a Request (`requests.plan_id`, giving account and Character), created in the last 30 days. A plan stored by `put_plan` without `requestId` and local Quick plans have no owner and give no wins in 0.10. Reason: we only count what we can attribute.
- D196 Q: When is it detected? A: in `ingestSnapshot`, after the Sections are stored: for each stored Section of the kinds above, the `added` ids of `sectionChange` (never an `initial` or `full` change, never a Section version change) are matched against the open plans' targets of that Account; the change `at` must be after the plan's `createdAt`. Reason: the first upload of mounts/achievements after 0.10 must not count 500 old mounts as wins.
- D197 Q: Storage? A: a new Player history kind **`win`** (history table, `HISTORY_KINDS` + "win"): `account`, `character`, `realm`, `at` (game change time), `ref` = `"mount:ID"` etc., body `{ planId, requestId, kind, id, token, planTitle, category }`. One win per Account and `ref`, ever (checked before insert). Forget my data erases it with the rest of the history. Reason: history already has account, character, time and deletion; no new table.
- D198 Q: MCP? A: new tool **`list_wins`** (`account?`, `character?`, `since?`, `limit` default 100): `{ wins[], totals: { mount, ach, toy, pet, item, quest }, days: [{ day: "YYYY-MM-DD" (UTC), wins[] }] }`. `get_player_history` lists `win` entries too. Reason: the weekly feed for the site later is a read of this, nothing new to store.
- D199 Q: Show it to the player in 0.10? A: **no** player-facing UI in 0.10 (no Companion line, no addon line). Data from 0.10.0 on, display later (site or Companion), as the owner said. Reason: minimal; the history must exist first; no new surface to design now.

## F. What a reward really gives

The 0.9 plan "Cage Hunt in Tanaan" listed three mounts and the Rattling Iron Cage `{item:128025}` (id from that plan) side by side; it read as if all four drop.

- D200 Q: Plan format? A: new optional plan field **`contains`**: `{ "<reward token>": ["<token>", ...] }`. The key must be a single `{item:ID}` token that is in plan `rewards` or a step's `rewards`; values 1-6 single tokens of kind mount, toy, pet or item. At most 2 containers per plan; values count toward the 12-token limit of goal plus details. Invalid key or value -> warning, dropped. `rewards` stays an array of strings. Added to the MCP `planSchema` (it is `.strict()`), `plan.schema.json`, `encodeUnb1`, Companion `render_plan_body`, addon `ValidatePlan`. Reason: additive; a 0.9 addon/Companion ignores it and shows the old list.
- D201 Q: On the card? A: a container reward line reads `[Rattling Iron Cage] · may contain 1 of 3 mounts` / `· может выпасть: 1 из 3 маунтов` on **one line** (closed card when it is the first reward); the open card adds one muted line under it with the content links. Contents the Account already has (live: `C_MountJournal`, `PlayerHasToy`, `C_PetJournal`) are left out of the count and the links; all owned -> "· you have all of them". Reason: one line says what drops and what you get; no extra rows on a closed card.
- D202 Q: Plan rewards order? A: put_plan description: "a container (cage, chest, bag) that drops is the reward; what it may hold goes in `contains`. Never list the possible contents as rewards themselves." The server warns (does not drop) when a plan lists 2+ mounts as rewards and an item reward without `contains`. Reason: the Planner fixes it from the warning.
- D203 Q: Suggestions? A: unchanged in 0.10 (ideas carry up to 3 rewards from the catalog, no containers there). Reason: the problem was in Planner plans only.

## G. Steps in any order

Today `Session:Skip` moves a step to the end and a second skip drops it (Session.lua:173); the card has only Done and Skip. In "Cage Hunt" the four rares can be done in any order, so Skip was used to look at the next one and then dropped it.

- D204 Q: Plan format? A: optional task field **`group`** (1-24 chars `[a-z0-9-]`): consecutive tasks with the same `group` form a **Step group** done in any order. A group needs at least 2 tasks; a non-consecutive repeat or a single-task group -> warning, `group` dropped from those tasks. No plan-level flag. Reason: real plans mix "fly to Tanaan" (first), "any of 4 rares" (middle), "open the cage" (last).
- D205 Q: On the card? A: when the current step is in a group with 2+ open steps, the meta line shows `‹  2 of 4 · any order  ›` with two small arrow buttons (`IconChevron`, which points left, D166; the right one rotated by pi). An arrow shows the previous/next open step of the group: it rotates the head block of `state.tasks` inside the group only; nothing is skipped or counted, the waypoint follows the shown step. Sequential plans look exactly as in 0.9. Reason: owner asked for flipping without dropping; no new art.
- D206 Q: Skip in a group? A: Skip = "won't do": the step goes to `skipped` at once (no move to the end). Tooltip `TIP_SKIP_GROUP` "Won't do this one. It leaves the plan." / "Не буду. Шаг уйдёт из плана." Outside a group Skip is unchanged. Reason: flipping covers "later", so Skip can mean only one thing there.
- D207 Q: Auto-complete? A: `CheckCurrent` checks every open step of the current group, not only the shown one, for game-truth kinds (quest, vignette, mount, toy, pet, achievement, criteria, currency, item); `proximity` and `zone` count only for the shown step; manual needs Done. A step finished off-screen gives the normal Echo and the card stays on the shown step. Reason: kill rare 3 while looking at rare 1, and it is still ticked.
- D208 Q: Keybinds, buttons? A: no new binding, no new action button; the arrows exist only in groups. Reason: fewer buttons.
- D209 Q: Planner rule? A: put_plan description: "Steps that can be done in any order (several rares, treasures, dailies in one zone) share a `group`. Give each grouped step a `complete` the game can check (quest or vignette for rares); a manual step in a group works but needs Done." Reason: groups are only good when they tick themselves.
