# Not sure what to do next in WoW?

A full quest log, another evening in WoW, and nothing you feel like starting. Bory, Unbored's ginger cat, offers an idea and a quick plan so you can get going.

## What works with the addon alone

- Pick an idea from your calendar or quest log, including a holiday or an unfinished quest chain.
- Start a quick plan in the game, with one step at a time and a map pin. **Show more** opens the full plan.
- See a short item tip when something matters to your plan, collection or an unfinished quest. Drop an item on Bory to see what he knows.
- Click through choices without typing. Bory hides in combat and can fold down to a small portrait.

**Retail only. The addon is not for WoW Forever or Classic yet.**

## Optional: the Windows Companion

Calendar and quest-log ideas, quick plans and item tips work without a desktop app. For proper plans, ideas from your other characters and the **Also:** item line showing where else you have an item, get the optional [Windows Companion](https://playunbored.com/install). That page has a one-click Windows download and setup help. Your PC needs a one-time OK from the Unbored team before proper plans start.

The Companion installs and updates the addon. When a new plan is waiting, Bory offers one **Refresh**; **Later** leaves it for your next login. The catalog download contains only the addon. The Companion is a separate download.

## Data

Bory reads your own characters and game account only:

- Character name and realm, race, faction, language, level and experience, class, specialization and talents, item level, gold, hearth location and whether you belong to a guild.
- Gear, bags, currencies, professions, recipes, your bank and warband bank; active and completed quests, quest chains, reputations, renown and paragon progress.
- Achievements and unfinished achievement progress, mounts, pets, toys, appearance counts, raid and dungeon lockouts, Mythic+ score and keystone, Great Vault progress and calendar holidays.
- Time played, recent zones and time spent there, deaths and level-ups; whether TomTom, Auctionator, TSM or Bagnon is loaded; items and plan links Bory could not explain or resolve, and cleaned addon error notes.

Game data is read after login, after changes settle, and while the relevant bank or profession screen is open, never in combat. Values the game marks secret are discarded. Bory does not read other players' names, chat, whispers, mail senders or messages, group or guild lists, or the combat log. No Battle.net password is needed.

The addon itself never goes online. WoW saves its addon data when you log out, switch characters or reload the interface. If you use the Companion, it sends changed game data to Unbored, along with plan requests, their character context and plan progress. A request is saved when you choose **Send now**, Refresh or log out. With sharing on, cleaned error notes include where the addon failed, its version and how often. Unbored uses the data for your plans, ideas and item advice, including your other characters. The Companion also identifies this PC by its name; your game account is linked using a salted hash of its folder name.

Sharing starts on. To reduce it, right-click Bory, open **Settings** and untick **Share game data to make Bory smarter**. This keeps character details, gear, the quest log and recent sessions for plans; the Companion can still send those and your plan requests. Error notes stay on your PC. Other characters' previously saved data is not erased by changing this setting. To use Bory with no Companion transfers, quit the Companion and use the addon alone.

To erase data already sent, open **Advanced** in the Companion, choose **Forget my data** and confirm. This also stops that PC sending more until you choose **Connect again**. Local addon files stay on your PC. [Read what Unbored keeps and what erasure covers](https://github.com/hmbanan666/wow-unbored/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Install and start

1. Install Unbored for Retail through this catalog, or close WoW and extract the addon zip into `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`.
2. Start Retail, click Bory on the left of your screen and pick an idea.
3. Right-click Bory for **Settings**, including data sharing and item lines.
