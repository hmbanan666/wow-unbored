# 0093. One solo-first guide per class

Status: accepted (2026-11-09)

## Context

"<class> leveling guide", "talents" and "build" are searched for all nine classes. Wowhead and Icy Veins publish leveling and endgame guides for all 27 specs plus talent calculators. We cannot out-cover them spec by spec, and the site's angle is solo play and the next step.

## Decision

One guide per class, built around solo leveling in its strongest solo spec, with talents explained in words (row, level, why) rather than a copied calculator, resource habits (shards, pet food, mana), what to buy and when, and our own screenshots when they exist (pages may ship text-only first). Warlock first (the owner plays Affliction and can supply real talent choices and screenshots), Hunter pets second, then Mage and Paladin. Endgame and raid specs are out of scope until raids open.

## Alternatives

- One guide per spec: rejected, three times the work for pages where we have no edge.
- A talent calculator: rejected, it is a database feature and Wowhead already has one.

## Consequences

Class pages target the class-level queries and link to the solo and leveling tier guides. Two class pages ship this week at most.
