# 0096. Game-specific imports and a Forever recipe catalog

Status: accepted (2026-11-11)

Following [0094](0094-forever-port-professions-and-character-history.md) T3 and [0095](0095-forever-professions-and-character-page.md), Game DB rows, builds and watches carry `game` (existing rows default to `retail`), `wow` and `wowf` import independently with Retail health fields preserved and a `forever` block added; Forever imports only SkillLine, SkillLineAbility, SpellReagents, ItemSparse, ItemEffect, SpellEffect, TradeSkillCategory and SpellName, plus the necessary ItemXItemEffect bridge because this build's ItemEffect has no item id.
A materialized `game.profession_recipes` catalog is published atomically with its build, keyed by game/build/profession/spell, restricted to the twelve real primary/secondary professions and categorized abilities, storing English names (null where absent from SpellName), raw AcquireMethod, skill-ups, reagents, crafted items and teaching-item links; orange starts at MinSkillLineRank, yellow at TrivialSkillLineRankLow, green at ceil((Low + High) / 2), grey at High, preserving unusual client thresholds without clamping, and [33 client examples](../forever-recipe-evidence.md) show AcquireMethod 0 in both 20 item-linked and 13 unlinked trainer-step candidates (observed, unconfirmed, not proof of trainer availability).
MCP `get_profession_catalog(game, skillLine)` exposes those rows and the private character page shows known N of M plus missing recipes by category, name, minimum skill and current colour, retains and notes known ids outside the catalog, and keeps known-only Retail and empty-catalog guidance until a catalog exists; sources, places, routes, history and public Forever compatibility claims remain T4-T7.
