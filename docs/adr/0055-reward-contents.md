# 0055. Reward contents

Status: accepted

A Plan may say what a container reward may hold: `contains` maps one `{item:ID}` reward to 1 to 6 mount, toy, pet or item tokens (at most 2 containers). The card shows the container on one line, "· may contain 1 of 3 mounts", leaves out contents the Account already has, and lists the content links on one muted line when the card is open; Suggestions stay as they are (D200-D203). This amends [0038](0038-one-plan-card.md).
