# 0068. Plan outcomes and shorter text

Status: accepted

Every Plan, including Surprise me and fun, has exactly one clear, near-guaranteed goal visible in its title, with Steps leading to a final Step that completes it: an incomplete achievement completable now, a reachable reputation/renown/level threshold, a guaranteed item or collectible, or a currency amount; planner submissions require a valid `outcome`, reject targets already held in the available snapshot, and accept missing data with a note, while chance rewards belong only in an optional `bonus` list (up to three reward tokens) and a clearly uncertain bonus line in a Step note. Surprise me chooses qualifying Ready now candidates through the existing tools; older stored Plans may lack `outcome`, and 0.11 safely ignores new target/bonus fields while keeping Steps and bonus notes (ADR 0066 delivery safeguards remain). Step titles are limited to 70 characters and 240 UTF-8 bytes for older addons, optional reasons to 80 bytes, Step notes to 400 bytes and Plan details to 200 bytes: excess text is shortened at a word with a warning, never a reason to reject the Plan; place names and points must come from game data or be omitted: correct quest points farther than 5% of the same map from a known quest point, drop unsupported travel points, and retain the starting-position rule from ADR 0065; unavailable tables remain explicitly unknown.
