# 0069. A lighter Plan card

Status: accepted

A new Plan opens collapsed with its title, Step pips and count for two or more Steps, current action, outcome chip and distance/time; expansion belongs to that Plan, its goal and details appear once in the expanded header, and the current Step note has a separate More toggle. The outcome chip reads progress from the player's game on relevant events, defers reads in combat, shows done when reached and keeps a label without a bar when data is unknown; bonuses remain small and explicitly uncertain, and older Plans retain their reward chip. A destination on another continent shows that continent from the game map's parent chain and defers the waypoint until a zone change places the player there; this amends the card presentation and Account-wide expansion in [0038](0038-one-plan-card.md).
