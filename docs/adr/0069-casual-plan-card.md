# 0069. A lighter Plan card

Status: accepted

A new Plan opens as one card: title with a reward icon, live progress directly below, current action with inline Step count and time, labelled Done/Skip buttons and one More/Less toggle for every explanation; expansion belongs to that Plan, and repeated title, goal and reward text is omitted. The unframed progress bar reads progress from the player's game on relevant events, defers reads in combat, shows done when reached and keeps a label without a bar when data is unknown; bonuses remain small and explicitly uncertain, and older Plans retain their reward chip. A destination on another continent shows that continent from the game map's parent chain and defers the waypoint until a zone change places the player there; this amends the card presentation and Account-wide expansion in [0038](0038-one-plan-card.md).

Live progress counts belong to the bar, not generated title, goal or Step prose; old note expansion state is ignored, and long notes keep their scroll area.
