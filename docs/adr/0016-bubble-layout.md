# 0016. Bubble layout

Status: accepted

No glow ring; the avatar (about 48-56 px) and the Bubble have separate scale settings. The Bubble always shows its full text, a small muted meta line on top ("1/5 · ~12 min"), and one action bar of same-style icon buttons with tooltips. Collapse is a chevron to a "..." pill that a click restores; Step changes animate; at most three Bubbles, older ones fade after 90 s; "New plan" is always reachable and an old Plan is offered to continue or replace on login (D20-D27).
