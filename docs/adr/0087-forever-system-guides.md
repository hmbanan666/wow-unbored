# 0087. WoW Forever system guides: one page per searched system

Status: accepted (2026-11-09)

## Context

Autocomplete shows strong, specific demand for Forever's own systems: camping (buffs, features, campfire), the Cozy Sleeping Bag (with separate Horde and Alliance queries), Legacy talents and points, and the Experience Preset (Enhanced or Classic). The beginner guide mentions these in passing. Competitors give the quest steps; few explain how the systems fit an evening of play.

## Decision

Write four pages in one batch: camping, Cozy Sleeping Bag, Legacy talents, Enhanced vs Classic preset. The sleeping bag gets its own page rather than a camping section, because its query and its faction variants are strong on their own; the two pages link each other. Each page has a table (features and buffs, both factions' steps with coordinates, Legacy points by source, preset settings), a FAQ, and the "evening routine" angle (campfire, sleeping bag, food and Well Rested together). Coordinates and numbers need two agreeing sources or our own screenshot; where sources disagree (for example the sleeping bag buff duration), the page says so until checked in game.

## Alternatives

- One long "Forever systems" page: rejected, it cannot rank for four different primary queries.
- Fold everything into the beginner guide: rejected for the same reason; the beginner guide links to the four pages instead.

## Consequences

Four new guides in the systems group of the hub. They need in-game screenshots for the buff and Legacy windows; without them the pages ship text-only.
