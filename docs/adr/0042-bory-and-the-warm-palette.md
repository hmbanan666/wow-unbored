# 0042. Bory and the warm palette

Status: accepted

The mascot is Bory, a ginger cat (Russian: Бори), in the addon and the Companion; Wisp's art is removed, and code names inside may still say `wisp` ([BRAND.md](../../BRAND.md)). His portrait is layers: a disc, his bust, and a thin gold ring on top; a mood (idle, thinking, waiting for a long wait, happy) swaps only the bust, and the bob moves all layers together so the ring never slides. Every colour comes from one warm palette (gold, highlight, burgundy, dark warm backgrounds, cream), `ns.PALETTE` in the addon and CSS variables in the Companion, with no teal. This amends [0004](0004-wisp-first-ui.md).
