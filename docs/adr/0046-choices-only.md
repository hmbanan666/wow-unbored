# 0046. Choices only, no free text

Status: accepted (amended by D214 in 0.10)

The player never types: Ask, the "nearby" question box, the Something else wish and its minutes field are gone, and every plan starts from a pick (an idea chip, a Preset, or Surprise me) or an item dropped on Bory (D122-D125). There is no time budget anywhere in the addon; the Server still reads `budget` and `custom` from 0.8 Requests. This supersedes the Ask parts of [0004](0004-wisp-first-ui.md) and [0014](0014-ask-is-core.md) and amends [0037](0037-no-time-budget.md).

Amended in 0.10 (D214): the `/unbored import` paste window and the `import`/`export` commands are gone; the Companion is the only way a Plan reaches the game.
