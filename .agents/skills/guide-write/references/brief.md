# Brief and evidence ledger

Fill this before drafting. It is working editorial evidence, not another content store.

- **Reader and intent:** "I am choosing my first solo character; which tradeoff will I live with?" is usable. "Write an SEO-optimized best-class article" is not a brief.
- **Game and scope:** Forever or Retail, activity, level range, assumptions about gear/resources, source build/date. Distinguish source data from a live-client check.
- **Answer:** one defensible recommendation and the strongest alternative; what condition reverses it?
- **Evidence ledger:** claim → exact source URL and relevant section → source date/build → actual UTC access date → fact / forecast / editorial inference / unresolved. An HTTP 200 alone does not verify a claim: read the body and follow material qualifications.
- **Reader sequence:** opening answer → two or three consequential choices → a next action. Use the structure that answers this question, not a fixed quota of sections or words.
- **Search promise:** proposed title/description; the actual question they promise to answer. Inspect neighboring URLs and choose useful internal next steps. Search volume and ranking claims need evidence of their own.
- **Exclusions:** unknown prices, unsupported timings, disputed recipes, untested outcomes. Identify the missing evidence that would let an editor add each later.

## Reject or narrow

"Give the cheapest Enchanting route using launch prices" fails if no current price sample and verified recipe quantities exist. Offer a decision guide to learning the profession and choosing between selling and disenchanting; keep the URL if that is the existing page, and remove a misleading leveling-route promise.

A class forecast can support "start your comparison here"; it cannot establish a measured fastest 1–60 run. Two sites repeating one datamined table are one evidence chain, not two independent tests.

## Evidence hierarchy

Use official game announcements for availability and rules. Use the appropriate game's client data or a transparent database for specific mechanics, preserving its build and unresolved fields. A community experiment needs its method and sample; a simulator needs its assumptions. A tier-list letter alone supports no player recommendation. Record commercial guides' provenance and corroborate their consequential claims. Never transfer Retail ids, Classic recipes or beta prices into Forever as live facts.
