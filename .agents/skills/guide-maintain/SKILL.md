---
name: guide-maintain
description: Audit, rewrite or refresh an existing Unbored guide for search intent, reader interest, source drift, inline explanations and metadata. Use after patches, source changes, reader feedback or a scheduled editorial review.
---

1. Read [the publication flow](../../../docs/content-authoring.md) and [the editorial standard](../../../docs/adr/0081-search-led-editorial-standard.md). Export/read the published document, metadata and rendered HTML; record its URL, version and sources before editing. Existing guides adopt this standard when next touched.
2. Refresh **search research** before rewriting, using the [brief and evidence ledger](../guide-write/references/brief.md): one primary query, 3–8 secondary queries from Google autocomplete, People also ask and the top five results, and intent. Log actual searches and dates. Compare neighboring guides to preserve URLs and distinct query ownership; flag missing search evidence.
3. Classify defects with every row of the [rubric and examples](references/rubric.md). Record failed dimensions and a representative before/after change. Check the brief against authored `table` and `faq` blocks and their rendered HTML, following the publication flow (ADR 0083).
4. Reopen sources for affected claims and log new evidence. Preserve old access dates for inaccessible evidence, disclose gaps and remove or narrow claims that cannot stand. Record changed facts separately from advice. Preserve verified specifics; label each forecast once at its scope and retain distinct disputed mechanics locally.
5. Rewrite around the query, with a concrete opening fact and answer in the first two sentences. Apply the rubric's interest, length, banned-pattern, links, metadata and alt-text checks. Guide visuals use only our own screenshots; otherwise omit images. No Wowhead images; compression or format conversion only, no other edits.
6. Run content validation and the publication flow's repeatable HTML audit. Check term links with keyboard, touch and scripts disabled; inspect heading order, mobile overflow and readable body text. Passing structure does not establish editorial quality or a search position.
7. Save a new database version through the authorized flow. Use runtime UTC publication timestamps only for substantive edits; record evidence checks honestly. Deliver the refreshed brief, change rationale, remaining gaps, validation and rollback notes. Every applicable rubric dimension must pass, including visible tables and FAQ answers. Blockers leave the draft unpublished.
